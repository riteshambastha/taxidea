DISTRICT CODES
==============
District codes vary by county. You'll need to find the specific
district code for the town you're targeting.

For Monmouth County (13):
- Marlboro = 28 (currently configured)
- (Other districts require lookup in the data files)

HOW TO FIND DISTRICT CODES:
1. grep '^[county_code]' modiv-2025/[County].txt | less
2. Look at the first 4 digits: [CC][DD] where CC=county, DD=district
3. Check the town name in the address field

Example:
grep '^1328' modiv-2025/Monmouth.txt | head -1
Shows records for County 13, District 28 (Marlboro)
